The datasets are bundled to reproduce the application configurations and
computational tables. They are not covered by the repository's MIT software
license. Their underlying classifications and observations retain the rights
and attribution requirements of their original sources. Confirm redistribution
permission and add archive-specific provenance before publishing a permanent
public release.
