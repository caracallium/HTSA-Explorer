HTSA-Explorer
Hierarchical Time Series Abstraction

Abstract a forest of time series
into k subtrees that matter

HTSA scans a hierarchy of time series, scores every branch for temporal coherence, extracts the k most representative subtrees, and collapses everything else — producing a compact, navigable summary tree of your data.

STEP 1

Parse the hierarchy

GraphML nodes carry raw series; edges define the tree. Multi-parent conflicts are resolved by temporal affinity into a clean directed forest.

STEP 2

Score similarity

Pairwise sim(x,u) between each root and its descendants — choose FDS, DTW, LCSS, MSM or Euclidean variants.

STEP 3

Select k subtrees

Maximize the quality score g(S) over disjoint subtrees — via fast Path-greedy or exact Optimal-Search (DP over a Pareto frontier).

STEP 4

Abstract

Winning subtrees collapse into supernodes S_·; unimportant chains compress into connectors — yielding the summary tree G′.

OBJECTIVE — SUBTREE QUALITY
g(S) = Σu∈S sim(u, x) · Σu∈S val(u) |S|
high temporal coherence × high total value, per node — HTSA picks k disjoint subtrees maximizing Σ g(S)
